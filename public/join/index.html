Join us

We are two people. The next few will shape what this becomes.

Who we are looking for

People who notice when something does not add up, and go and find out why instead of rounding it off. People who would rather say “the data stops here” than make something up to fill the gap.

We do not have job titles yet. These are the kinds of people we want to hear from.

The kinds of work

From the sea
Master mariners, navigating officers, VTS operators, surveyors, claims and P&I people. You know what a real track looks like and what a dispute really turns on.
From the signal
GNSS and radio engineers, SAR and remote-sensing people, anyone who has decoded AIS or ADS-B for fun.
From the data
Engineers who build pipelines that keep the raw truth, statisticians who measure their own error, and naval architects who know what a hull can and cannot do.

How we work

Small, in Gujarat, and in the open: our methods are published and our code is checked by people outside the company. We write down what we got wrong and fix it where people will see it.

It is early. That means real ownership and real uncertainty, and we will be straight with you about both.

How to apply

Email your CV and a few lines on something you found that did not add up, and what you did about it. No cover letter needed.
