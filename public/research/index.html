Ship manoeuvring and resistance models under sparse AIS sampling: what can be predicted, identified and bounded

Vishmayraj Zala, Kathan Patel. Under review, 2026.

Code and data on Zenodo

What can AIS reports, seconds to minutes apart, tell ship physics? We measured it on 47.7 million reports and ships the models had not seen.

Findings
TurnsOn course changes of 45° or more, standard turn models place the ship about 22% worse at the 95th percentile than simply averaging the two reports.
SteeringA hull’s steering constants can’t be identified from AIS, because AIS carries no rudder angle.
SpeedA Holtrop–Mennen speed screen is crossed by 4 of 8,472 ships up to Froude number 0.45, and by 181 of 199 above it.
SoBetween sparse reports, ship physics is good for bounds, not for filling in the gaps.
