Research

How far ship physics holds between two AIS reports.

Urania, Johann Theodor de Bry, 1601, Art Institute of Chicago

Ship manoeuvring and resistance models under sparse AIS sampling: what can be predicted, identified and bounded

Vishmayraj Zala, Kathan Patel. Under review, 2026. 47,739,929 reports, Baltic and North Sea.

Findings
TurnsOn sharp turns, textbook models place the ship about 22% worse than averaging the two reports.
SteeringA hull’s steering constants cannot be read from AIS. It carries no rudder angle.
Speed4 of 8,472 ships cross a Holtrop–Mennen speed screen up to Froude number 0.45.
SoBetween sparse reports, physics is good for bounds, not for filling gaps.
