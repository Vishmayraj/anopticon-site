About

The name, and why we picked it.

A watchtower with glass walls.

Bentham’s panopticon is a prison with a tower in the middle: the guard sees every cell and no prisoner sees the guard. Take away the “pan” and you get Anopticon. We kept the tower and took out the secret. Whatever we conclude about a ship comes with the data and the method, so anyone can climb up and look.

The word is older than the company. In 2009 a project in Venice used it to map the city’s CCTV cameras. We are the maritime one.

an-OP-ti-kon

Four syllables, stress on the second. Founded in 2026, in Gujarat.
