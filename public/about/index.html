Why Anopticon

The panopticon is a prison where one hidden guard sees everyone. Take away the “pan”: the tower stays, but anyone can look inside.

Question. Investigate. Verify.

Ships, aircraft and sensors all report where they are. More of those reports are wrong, and few people check.

We test each report against what the world allows, and hand over the data so anyone can check us.
