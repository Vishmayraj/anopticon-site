Argus

Navies and coast guards. Next, once the sea work has earned it.

The story

Argus Panoptes had a hundred eyes and never closed them all. Hermes lulled every one of them to sleep anyway.

Watching everything is not the same as knowing what is true.

What it would do

Find ships that go dark, ships that meet at sea without saying so, and ships whose identity does not match their hull, by putting AIS next to radar satellites and radio.

Built on the same core as Nereus, with the same rule: say what the evidence supports and where it stops.

Where it stands

A radar-first scan that looks for ships AIS does not explain already runs in test. Argus starts properly after Nereus has paying customers and a published method with its error rates.
