Aether

Aircraft. Later.

The story

Aether is the bright upper air of the Greek sky. In Bruegel’s engraving a warship holds its course while Icarus falls out of the sky above it.

What it would do

Aircraft broadcast where they are over ADS-B, and those positions are hit by the same jamming and spoofing as ships. The method that finds many ships reporting one impossible place applies to aircraft almost unchanged.

Where it stands

An intention, not a product. It comes after the sea and the navies, and only if the core has proved it carries over.
