Design partners

We are working with three to five early partners on real cases. If a ship’s position matters to your work, this is how it goes.

Who it is for

Charterers and owners with a disputed voyage. Claims handlers and P&I correspondents. Admiralty lawyers and the experts they instruct. Anyone who has had to argue about where a ship was, and wished the data could be checked by both sides.

What you get, what we ask

Your cases, looked at
We work up to three of your real cases into records: what the positions show, what they cannot show, and the data behind both.
A say in the product
You tell us what a record has to contain to be useful in your work, and we build that, not what we imagined.
What we ask
Real cases under NDA, a small fee per case that is credited in full if you carry on with us, and the outcome of each case once it closes.

One thing to know

We are independent. Our fee never depends on the outcome, and a record says what the data shows even when that hurts the side that asked for it. That is the only way a record is worth showing to the other side.

Everything is covered by a written agreement on scope, data rights, confidentiality and liability before any work starts.

How to start

Send us the ship (name or MMSI), the dates, and one line on what is in dispute. We will reply with whether the data can say anything useful.
