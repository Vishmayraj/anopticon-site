Records of what actually happened.

Anopticon tests what machines report against what the physical world allows. We start at sea. Ships broadcast their own positions every few seconds, and those broadcasts are now jammed, spoofed and faked.

Bring us a case

What we do

Every large ship reports its position over AIS, a radio system with no signature on its messages. The position comes from the ship’s satellite receiver, and that receiver can be jammed or fooled. Ports, insurers, charterers, lawyers and navies all rely on those positions anyway.

We check them. Each report is tested against what that ship can physically do, against the ships around it, and against independent sources such as radar satellites. What we find goes into a record, with the data and the method, so the other side can check it too.
