Is that shipreally there?

Anopticon verifies the positions ships report. Every ship broadcasts where it is. Those broadcasts can be jammed, spoofed and forged, and everything downstream believes them.

Flags couldn’t lie from far away.

For a century and a half ships spoke in flags. To forge a ship’s signal you had to be standing on it.

Now they speak in radio. A ship’s position comes from a satellite signal, goes out over AIS, and is read by ports, insurers, sanctions desks and models that never see the sea. That signal can be faked from the shore, out of sight. That is data poisoning, and it is what we work on.
