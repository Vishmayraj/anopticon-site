Othello asked for proof and took a handkerchief. Most data is still trusted that way. We hand over the evidence and the means to check it.

Physics can’t be poisoned.

Projects

One question, asked of every machine that reports where it is. Sea first.

Project Nereus

The sea. Now.

Nereus, the old man of the sea. Hesiod calls him the one who is true and lies not. To get a straight answer from him you had to hold on while he became a lion, a serpent, water.

Ship data shifts shape the same way. Nereus is our work on the sea: what really happened, and the means for anyone to check it.

Project Argus

The watchers. Next.

Argus Panoptes had a hundred eyes and never closed them all. Hermes lulled every one to sleep and killed him anyway.

Watching everything is not the same as seeing what is true. Argus is for the navies and coast guards who watch the sea.

Project Aether

The sky. Later.

Aether, the bright upper air. Bruegel put a warship and a falling Icarus in one frame: sea and sky, each trusting what it is told.

Aircraft broadcast where they are too, and that record can be poisoned the same way. Aether takes the question up.
